Definitions and limitations

Positive balance = max(September bill, 0). Weighted utilization = total positive balance / total credit limits; values over 100% are preserved. Default rate = next-month default labels / accounts. Demographic fields are excluded from dashboard segmentation. Repayment codes −2, −1 and 0 are grouped as no positive delay without inventing undocumented meanings. These historical Taiwanese accounts do not represent current Indian banking customers.
